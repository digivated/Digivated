# Solar icon set

Site icons in `src/lib/card-icons.mjs` come from **Solar** by 480 Design
(Bold Duotone and Linear styles), retrieved through the Iconify API.

- Source: https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736 and https://icon-sets.iconify.design/solar/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: path coordinates rounded to two decimals; a few glyphs (close, check) drawn to match.
